알파 12 이후의 업데이트입니다. 카지노에 함대 오토배틀러 [b]스텔라체스[/b]가 생겼고, 개인실에서 우리 사람들끼리 노는 [b]관저 파티[/b]를 열 수 있습니다. 집무실에는 [b]여론조사·국민투표·특별사면[/b], 여당·야당이 있는 [b]의회[/b], [b]로비[/b], [b]원시문명 관측[/b], [b]총독에게 행성 맡기기[/b]가 들어왔습니다.

[*]30라운드 · 목숨 3 · 시간 제한 없음. 상점에서 함선을 사서 8칸 판에 배치하면 [b]실시간 전투[/b]가 펼쳐집니다(함선 그림, 사격·미사일·함재기·수리 연출, 1·2배속·건너뛰기).
[*][b]10개 종족 × 5척 = 함선 50척[/b](구매 비용 1~5). 같은 함선 3척이 모이면 별이 오릅니다.

[*][b]혼돈 모드[/b](라운드마다 변칙 규칙, 배당 ×1.5) · [b]하드 모드[/b](보스 능력치 ×2.5 — 그레이트 칸만 ×2, 일반 적 ×1.5, 피해 ×1.25, 후반 배당 증가, 점수 ×2). 최고 기록은 일반·하드 따로 남습니다.

[h2]🎉 관저 파티 (개인실)[/h2]
[list]
[*]사교회와 달리 [b]우리 사람들끼리[/b] 격식 없이 노는 자리입니다. 개인실의 「🎉 관저 파티」로 엽니다. 180일마다 한 번.
[*]손님 2~10명: 우리 지도자(통치자 제외) · 연인·배우자(비서 포함) · [b]타국 만찬에서 연락처를 주고받은 다른 나라 지도자[/b] 최대 2명(전쟁 중인 나라 제외).
[*]성향별 [b]관저 파티장 그림 8종[/b] 위에서 세 장면(손님 맞이 · 한창 · 늦은 밤)이 흘러갑니다. 장면마다 조명이 바뀌고, 손님들이 자리를 옮기며 수다를 떨고, 가끔은 서너 명이 모인 [b]무리[/b]가 생깁니다.
[*][b]소동 29가지[/b](술 대결, 계단 괴담, 소파 카드 게임, 술 쏟기…). 손님을 눌러 직접 말을 걸 수 있습니다(그때만 AI).
[*]마지막에 [b]단체 사진[/b]: 통치자가 가운데, 손님은 앞뒤 두 줄로. 사진은 그림으로 저장돼 [b]개인 일지 → 📷 사진첩[/b]에 모이고, 일지에 [b]다꾸처럼 사진을 붙일[/b] 수 있습니다(마스킹 테이프 인화지).
[*]효과: 손님 신뢰 +3, 함께 어울린 지도자끼리 관계 +, 통합력 +1%(360일), 전쟁·위기 중이면 함대 사격 속도·지상군 피해 +1%, 다른 나라 손님의 나라는 호감 +10. [i](모드 파일 변경)[/i]
[/list]

[h2]🗳️ 시민 정치 (집무실)[/h2]
[list]

[*][b]국민투표[/b]: 정책을 선포할 때 국민투표에 부칠 수 있습니다(2년에 1번). 통과하면 2년 동안 안정도 +5·민심 +3. 부결인데 강행하면 1년 안정도 −5·민심 −4 — [b]민주제에서는 부결되면 강행할 수 없습니다[/b].
[*][b]특별사면[/b]: 국경일·행성 기념일이 든 달에만, 1년에 1번. 경범 사면 · 일반 사면 · 대사면(안정도 +2/+4/+6, 범죄 +5/+10/+20, 1년). 정치범을 포함하면 파벌 반응이 갈립니다.
[*]정부 형태에 따라 달라집니다: 독재·제정은 「관제 조사」(늘 80% 이상)·「요식 투표」(늘 통과, 외국 언론이 비웃음), 단일 의식 제국에는 없습니다. [i](모드 파일 변경)[/i]
[/list]

[*][b]로비[/b]: 이익단체가 찾아옵니다. 「공식 지원」은 국고로 들어오고 공개되며, 「뒷돈」은 금고로 들어오지만 나중에 들킬 수 있습니다(들키면 2년 안정도 −6·민심 −6). 공개 고발도 할 수 있습니다. [i](모드 파일 변경)[/i]

[h1]SLLM Alpha 12.1 — StellaChess · House Party · Civic Politics & Parliament · Pre-FTL Observation · Entrust Planets to Governors (0.1.0-alpha.12.1)[/h1]

An update following Alpha 12. The casino now has a fleet auto-battler, [b]StellaChess[/b], and you can throw a [b]house party[/b] for your own people from the private room. The Office gains [b]polls, referendums and pardons[/b], a [b]parliament[/b] with government and opposition, [b]lobbying[/b], [b]pre-FTL observation[/b], and [b]entrusting planets to governors[/b].

⚠️ [b]Mod files changed.[/b] Update this mod (Workshop) and the app together, then fully restart the game.

[h2]♟️ StellaChess (Casino)[/h2]
[list]
[*]A single-player [b]fleet auto-battler[/b] inside the casino station. The buy-in comes from your vault and you are paid by how far you get (the house has a slight edge). No game effects.
[*]30 rounds · 3 lives · no time limit. Buy ships in the shop, place them on an 8-slot board and watch a [b]real-time battle[/b] (ship art, gunfire, missiles, strike craft, repairs; 1×/2× speed or skip).
[*][b]10 species × 5 ships = 50 ships[/b] (cost 1–5). Three copies of a ship raise its star level.
[*]Three kinds of synergy: [b]species[/b] (tiers 2/3/4/5) · [b]9 weapon classes[/b] (mass driver, autocannon, railgun, laser, plasma, lance, missile, torpedo, strike craft) · [b]5 roles[/b] (picket, line, artillery, raider, carrier; tiers 2/4/6).
[*][b]Items[/b]: 8 components → 36 completed items (TFT-style recipes). Drag and drop to equip; check the icon recipe book. Picks come on set rounds, and winning a regular round has a [b]20% chance to salvage a component[/b] from the wreckage.
[*]Materials: 5 per round + [b]interest (1 per 10, up to 5)[/b] + 1 per win + streak bonus.
[*][b]6 bosses[/b]: 5 Great Khan's Horde · 10 Dimensional Horror · 15 Ether Drake · 20 [b]the Unbidden or the Prethoryn[/b] (50% per run) · 25 Cetana · 30 the [b]Late-Game Lag Avatar[/b] (the screen pretends to freeze: "(Not Responding)"…). After beating any boss you may retreat at any time for that stage's payout.
[*][b]Chaos mode[/b] (a twist each round, payout ×1.5) · [b]Hard mode[/b] (bosses ×2.5 — Great Khan ×2, regular foes ×1.5, damage ×1.25, bigger late payouts, score ×2). Best records are kept separately for normal and hard.
[/list]

[h2]🎉 House Party (Private Room)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Unlike the social gathering, this is an off-the-record night [b]with your own people[/b]. Open it with "🎉 House party" in the private room. Once every 180 days.
[*]2–10 guests: your leaders (not the ruler) · lovers and spouses (the secretary included) · up to 2 [b]foreign leaders you exchanged contacts with at foreign banquets[/b] (not from empires at war with you).
[*]Three scenes (arrivals · in full swing · late night) play out over [b]8 party-venue paintings by ethic[/b]. The lighting changes each scene, guests move around and chat, and now and then a [b]group[/b] of three or four gathers.
[*][b]29 incidents[/b] (drinking contests, ghost stories on the stairs, card games on the sofa, spilled drinks…). Click a guest to talk to them (AI only then).
[*]It ends with a [b]group photo[/b]: the ruler in the middle, guests in two staggered rows. The photo is saved as a picture in [b]Personal journal → 📷 Photo album[/b], and you can [b]paste photos into journal entries[/b] scrapbook-style (taped prints).
[*]Effects: guest trust +3, leaders who mingled grow closer, unity +1% (360 days), +1% fire rate and army damage if held in war or crisis, +10 opinion from foreign guests' empires. [i](mod file change)[/i]
[/list]

[h2]🗳️ Civic Politics (Office)[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Polls[/b]: published every 3 months by each newspaper (each with a slight lean) and printed in the papers.
[*][b]Referendums[/b]: put a policy to a vote when you proclaim it (once every 2 years). A pass gives stability +5 and mood +3 for 2 years; forcing a rejected policy costs stability −5 for a year and mood −4 — [b]democracies cannot force a rejected policy[/b].
[*][b]Special pardons[/b]: only in a month with a national holiday or planet holiday, once a year. Petty · general · grand (stability +2/+4/+6, crime +5/+10/+20 for a year). Including political prisoners splits the factions.
[*]Depends on government: autocracies and empires get "staged polls" (always 80%+) and "rubber-stamp votes" (always pass, mocked abroad); gestalt empires have none of this. [i](mod file change)[/i]
[/list]

[h2]🏛️ Parliament · Audits · No-Confidence · Lobbying[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Government and opposition[/b]: 200 seats split by faction support. Without a majority you need a [b]coalition[/b], and you can hold [b]summits with the named opposition leader[/b] (AI, up to 5 lines). Floor fights happen now and then — defections, crossings, street protests. Autocracies and empires get a "rubber-stamp parliament".
[*][b]National audit[/b] (turn on in Settings, off by default): every 12 years an audit opens in January. The agenda comes from what actually happened in the last 12 years (AI), and your answers move stability, mood and the papers. Skipping it counts as a no-show.
[*][b]No-confidence[/b] (turn on in Settings, off by default): in free governments, approval below 25% for three months brings a motion. Defend yourself with a speech (AI-scored) or dissolve parliament; government seats count in the vote. If it passes: "lame duck" (stability −5, influence −20% for 3 years).
[*][b]Lobbying[/b]: interest groups come calling. "Official support" goes to the treasury and is public; "bribes" go to your vault but may come out later (stability −6, mood −6 for 2 years). You can also denounce them publicly. [i](mod file change)[/i]
[/list]

[h2]🔭 Pre-FTL Observation (Office)[/h2]
[list]
[*]See the age, awareness and observation posts of the primitive civilizations you know on one screen. It does not poll every month — it asks the game [b]only on first contact and when you open the screen[/b] (less game load).
[*][b]Observation reports[/b] (template text, or AI-written like a real report if you choose) · change log · [b]broadcast interception[/b] (industrial age and up: radio, TV, satellite; content changes with awareness).
[*][b]Interventions[/b]: gift of technology · plague medical aid · deliberate sighting · cover-up operation · change the post's mission.
[*][b]Voice from the Sky[/b]: speak to a primitive civilization directly. Each line raises awareness by its risk (a whisper … the whole world looks up), followed by aftermaths — prophets, madmen, sages.
[*]After uplift or conquest they remain as an empire that remembers what you did. [i](mod file change)[/i]
[/list]

[h2]🪐 Entrust Planets to Governors (Office → Planetary governors)[/h2]
[list]
[*]In "Entrust planets", hand a planet to its governor and the game's [b]planet automation[/b] builds buildings, districts and zones by one of [b]8 mandates[/b] the governor recommends (research, alloys, consumer goods, mining, energy, farming, urban & trade, fortress). Costs are the game's own.
