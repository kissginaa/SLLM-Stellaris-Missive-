[h1]SLLM 알파 12.22 — 양당 정치 · 인물 관계도 · 게임 부담 줄이기 · 시나리오 팩 지원 · 설치기 다국어 (0.1.0-alpha.12.22)[/h1]

알파 12.3 이후의 업데이트입니다. 민주정 · 과두정 · 기업 정부에 [b]두 거대 정당[/b]이 생겨 지지율 · 대정부 질문 · 선거전 · 총선이 돌아가고, 지도자 카드에서 [b]인물 관계도[/b]를 볼 수 있습니다. 매달 게임과 주고받던 확인을 [b]바뀔 때만[/b] 하도록 줄여 게임 부담을 덜었고, 첫 시나리오 팩 [b]「회랑의 삼파전」[/b]을 앱이 지원합니다.

⚠️ [b]모드 파일이 바뀌었습니다.[/b] 이 모드(창작마당)와 앱을 함께 최신으로 맞추고, 게임을 완전히 다시 시작해 주세요.

[h2]🏛️ 양당 정치 (집무실 「의회」 맨 위)[/h2]
[list]
[*]민주정 · 과두정 · 기업 정부에서만 켜집니다(독재 · 제정 · 군체 · 기계 지성은 없음). 의회의 파벌 의석 위에 [b]집권당[/b]과 [b]제1야당[/b]이 생기고, 파벌은 당 안의 계파가 됩니다. 당 이름은 제국 성향에 맞춰 붙습니다.
[*][b]정당 지지율[/b]이 매달 움직입니다. 국정 승인도를 천천히 따라가고 사건이 흔듭니다. 집권 첫 1년은 허니문, 오래 집권하면 피로가 쌓이고, 전쟁 초반에는 결집 · 2년이 넘으면 역풍이 붑니다. 추이 그래프로 볼 수 있습니다.
[*][b]야당이 먼저 움직입니다[/b]: 4년마다 [b]대정부 질문[/b]. 야당이 실제 상황(전쟁 · 불안 행성 · 낮은 승인도 · 날치기 · 과반 여부 등)을 짚어 묻고, 플레이어가 답을 직접 쓰면 AI가 국민의 눈으로 판정해 지지율에 반영합니다. 답변을 거부할 수도 있습니다(손해).
[*][b]총선[/b]: 민주정은 6년, 그 밖은 10년마다. 6달 전부터 [b]선거전[/b] — 공약 3개를 고르고(마감까지 안 고르면 당 지도부가 정함) 야당 대표와 [b]후보 토론[/b](발언 2번, AI 판정)을 합니다. 민주정은 지지율이 1.5배 크게 흔들립니다.
[*]총선 득표가 [b]의회 의석을 실제로 옮깁니다[/b](이긴 쪽이 조금 더 가져감). 지난 공약은 다음 총선 때 성적표가 되어, 지키면 득 · 어기면 손해입니다. 지난 총선 기록이 남습니다.
[*]AI는 대정부 질문 판정과 후보 토론에만 씁니다. 질문 · 공약 · 성명은 미리 쓴 틀 문장입니다.
[/list]

[h2]🕸️ 인물 관계도 (지도자 인물 카드)[/h2]
[list]
[*]지도자 인물 카드의 [b]「🕸 관계도」[/b]로 우리 지도자들의 관계를 한 화면에서 봅니다. 인물 카드 「인간관계」와 같은 규칙으로, 통치자와의 관계 · 공개된 관계 · 소개로 알게 된 관계 · 플레이어가 아는 갈등만 나옵니다.
[*][b]세력판[/b]: 통치자가 가운데, 진한 관계(연인 · 배우자 · 친구 · 라이벌)로 묶인 무리가 둘레에 섭니다. 통치자와 관계없는 무리와 홀로 선 인물은 선 없이 바깥쪽에 따로 섭니다(그래도 모두 나옵니다). 무리를 누르면 그쪽으로 날아갑니다.
[*]연인 · 배우자는 [b]같은 크기 초상화로 나란히[/b](♥) 섭니다. 통치자의 짝은 통치자와 같은 크기입니다.
[*][b]아는 사이(지인)[/b]도 가는 회색 선으로 이어집니다. 무리 사이의 지인 선은 각 무리에서 지인이 가장 많은 사람끼리 한 줄로 묶이고(「지인 n」), 인물을 누르면 그 사람의 선만 풀어서 보여 줍니다.
[*][b]인물 관계도[/b] 탭: 고른 인물을 가운데에 두고 바로 이어진 사람, 그다음 사람까지 둘레로 펼칩니다. 나온 사람끼리의 관계는 모두 선으로 그리고, 가운데를 가로지르는 선은 휘어 돌아갑니다. 마우스를 올린 인물의 선만 밝아집니다.
[*]게임에 묻는 것 없이 앱 기록만 읽습니다.
[/list]

[*]은하 위기 상황은 바뀔 때만 알리고, 1년에 한 번 다시 맞춥니다. [i](모드 파일 변경)[/i]
[*][b]세이브 다시 읽기[/b]: 세이브를 불러올 때 · 통치자가 바뀔 때 · 첫 접촉 · 우리 성향이나 권위가 바뀔 때 · 12달이 지났을 때만 다음 자동 저장을 읽습니다. 작전지휘실 지도는 늘 그 게임 폴더의 가장 새 세이브를 씁니다.
[*]큰 저장 파일 쓰기와 로그 찾기를 [b]작업 스레드[/b]로 옮겨 앱 화면이 덜 멈춥니다. 연대기는 모아서 씁니다.

[h1]SLLM Alpha 12.22 — Two-Party Politics · Relationship Chart · Lighter on the Game · Scenario Pack Support · Multilingual Installer (0.1.0-alpha.12.22)[/h1]

Updates since Alpha 12.3. Democratic, oligarchic and corporate empires now have [b]two major parties[/b] with support ratings, question time, campaigns and general elections, and the leader card gains a [b]relationship chart[/b]. Monthly checks between the app and the game now happen [b]only when something changes[/b], and the app supports the first scenario pack, [b]"Corridor Triangle"[/b].

⚠️ [b]Mod files changed.[/b] Update this mod (Workshop) and the app together, then fully restart the game.

[h2]🏛️ Two-party politics (top of the Office "Parliament")[/h2]
[list]
[*]Only for democratic, oligarchic and corporate governments (not dictatorial, imperial, hive mind or machine). A [b]governing party[/b] and a [b]main opposition[/b] sit on top of the parliament's faction seats, and factions become wings inside the parties. Party names follow the empire's ethics.
[*][b]Party support[/b] moves every month: it slowly follows government approval and events shake it. The first year in power is a honeymoon, long rule builds fatigue, and a war first rallies the public, then turns against you after two years. A trend graph shows it.
[*][b]The opposition moves first[/b]: [b]question time[/b] every 4 years. The opposition asks about the real situation (wars, worlds in unrest, low approval, rammed-through bills, a missing majority…). You write the answer yourself and the AI judges it through the public's eyes, which moves support. You may refuse to answer (at a cost).
[*][b]General elections[/b]: every 6 years in a democracy, every 10 otherwise. The [b]campaign[/b] opens 6 months before: pick 3 pledges (the party leadership picks if you don't) and face the opposition leader in a [b]debate[/b] (2 turns, judged by the AI). Support swings 1.5× harder in a democracy.
[*]The vote [b]really moves parliament seats[/b] (the winner gets a little extra). Past pledges become a report card at the next election: kept ones help, broken ones hurt. Past elections are recorded.
[*]The AI is only used to judge question time and debates. Questions, pledges and statements are pre-written templates.
[/list]

[h2]🕸️ Relationship chart (leader card)[/h2]
[list]
[*]Press [b]"🕸 Relationship chart"[/b] on a leader card to see all of our leaders' ties on one screen. Same rules as the card's "Relationships": ties with the ruler, public ties, ties learned through introductions, and conflicts you know about.
[*][b]Power board[/b]: the ruler in the centre, circles bound by close ties (partners, spouses, friends, rivals) around them. Circles and loners with no tie to the ruler stand apart on the outside with no line (everyone is still shown). Click a circle to fly to it.
[*]Partners and spouses stand [b]side by side at the same portrait size[/b] (♥). The ruler's partner is as large as the ruler.
[*][b]Acquaintances[/b] are joined by thin grey lines. Acquaintance lines between circles are bundled into one line between each circle's best-connected person ("n acq."); click a person to unbundle just their lines.
[*][b]Relationship chart[/b] tab: the chosen person in the centre, their direct ties and the next ring around them. Every tie among the people shown is drawn, lines that would cross the centre bend around it, and hovering a person lights only their lines.
[*]Reads only the app's records; nothing is asked of the game.
[/list]

[h2]⚙️ Lighter on the game — only when something changes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Monthly survey[/b]: agenda, ethics, enclave, faction, subject and public-mood data are sent only in months they change, and planets are no longer walked twice. [i](mod files changed)[/i]
[*][b]Monthly payments[/b] (deal tribute and monthly payments, rebel sponsorship, lend-lease): written into the mod once when agreed, then the game pays every month by itself. The app only hears about missed, broken or finished payments. After loading a save it only checks the payment still exists in the game; if not, it is over. [i](mod files changed)[/i]
[*]Mercenary discount settlement, Interstellar Council task progress, the vulnerable-state check, and the outcome of subject proxy wars and fleet levies (war over, fleet destroyed, lease ended) are [b]reported by the mod when they happen[/b]. The app double-checks once a year in January. [i](mod files changed)[/i]
[*]Schedule cooldowns are read only in the first month after loading, in months you open the schedule, and every 12 months.
[*]The galactic crisis status is sent only when it changes and re-synced once a year. [i](mod files changed)[/i]
[*][b]Save re-reads[/b]: the next autosave is read only after loading a save, a new ruler, a first contact, a change of our ethics or authority, or after 12 months. The War Room map always uses the newest save in that game folder.
[*]Writing large data files and searching logs moved to a [b]worker thread[/b], so the app freezes less. The chronicle is written in batches.
[/list]

## 🏛️ 양당 정치 (집무실 「의회」 맨 위)

## 🏛️ Two-party politics (top of the Office "Parliament")
- Only for democratic, oligarchic and corporate governments (not dictatorial, imperial, hive mind or machine). A **governing party** and a **main opposition** sit on top of the parliament's faction seats, and factions become wings inside the parties. Party names follow the empire's ethics.
- **Party support** moves every month: it slowly follows government approval and events shake it. The first year in power is a honeymoon, long rule builds fatigue, and a war first rallies the public, then turns against you after two years. A trend graph shows it.
- **The opposition moves first**: **question time** every 4 years. The opposition asks about the real situation (wars, worlds in unrest, low approval, rammed-through bills, a missing majority…). You write the answer yourself and the AI judges it through the public's eyes, which moves support. You may refuse to answer (at a cost).
- **General elections**: every 6 years in a democracy, every 10 otherwise. The **campaign** opens 6 months before: pick 3 pledges (the party leadership picks if you don't) and face the opposition leader in a **debate** (2 turns, judged by the AI). Support swings 1.5× harder in a democracy.
- The vote **really moves parliament seats** (the winner gets a little extra). Past pledges become a report card at the next election: kept ones help, broken ones hurt. Past elections are recorded.
- The AI is only used to judge question time and debates. Questions, pledges and statements are pre-written templates.

## 🕸️ Relationship chart (leader card)
- Press **"🕸 Relationship chart"** on a leader card to see all of our leaders' ties on one screen. Same rules as the card's "Relationships": ties with the ruler, public ties, ties learned through introductions, and conflicts you know about.
- **Power board**: the ruler in the centre, circles bound by close ties (partners, spouses, friends, rivals) around them. Circles and loners with no tie to the ruler stand apart on the outside with no line (everyone is still shown). Click a circle to fly to it.
- Partners and spouses stand **side by side at the same portrait size** (♥). The ruler's partner is as large as the ruler.
- **Acquaintances** are joined by thin grey lines. Acquaintance lines between circles are bundled into one line between each circle's best-connected person ("n acq."); click a person to unbundle just their lines.
- **Relationship chart** tab: the chosen person in the centre, their direct ties and the next ring around them. Every tie among the people shown is drawn, lines that would cross the centre bend around it, and hovering a person lights only their lines.
- Reads only the app's records; nothing is asked of the game.
