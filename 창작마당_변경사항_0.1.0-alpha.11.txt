===== 창작마당 (한국어, BBCode) =====
[h1]SLLM 알파 11 — 살아 있는 제국들 (0.1.0-alpha.11)[/h1]

이번 업데이트는 제국 사이의 관계를 더 살아 있게 만들었습니다. 주고받을 것을 묶어 흥정하는 외교 거래, 약탈자와의 흥정, 그레이트 칸의 특별 대우, 성간 임시회의의 과제가 생겼고, AI가 국경·전선·영유권 같은 게임 속 외교 사정을 알고 말합니다. 새 기능은 대부분 게임 자료와 앱 계산으로 움직여, 직접 대화하는 때가 아니면 AI를 쓰지 않습니다.

⚠️ [b]모드 파일이 바뀌었습니다.[/b] 이 모드(창작마당)와 앱을 함께 최신으로 맞추고, 게임을 완전히 다시 시작해 주세요.

[h2]🤝 외교 거래[/h2]
[list]
[*]집무실 「국정」에 [b]「외교 거래」[/b]가 생겼습니다. 통신 중인 일반 제국을 골라 주고받을 것을 한 번에 묶어 제안합니다. 외교 단말기에서 대화하다가 「거래 제안」 버튼으로 열 수도 있습니다.
[*][b]설득 확률[/b]: 항목을 담을 때마다 상대가 받아들일 확률과 그 이유(호감·신뢰·국력 차이·거래 저울 등)가 바로 보이고, 결정은 그 확률대로 정해집니다. 같은 달에 같은 상대를 다시 조르면 확률이 10%p씩 내려갑니다. 단말기에서 대화 중에 제안하면 상대가 결과에 맞춰 한마디 하고, 집무실에서 제안하면 AI 없이 결과만 나옵니다.
[*][b]묶음 거래[/b]: 자원(한 번에·매달), 국경 개방, 경쟁 관계 해제, 불가침 약속, 우리 전쟁에 참전을 함께 담을 수 있습니다. 외교 항목의 가치는 게임 연도가 지날수록 커집니다.
[*][b]조공 평화[/b]: 전쟁 중인 상대와 종전에 매달 지급을 묶어 평화를 삽니다. 매달 지급은 내는 쪽 월수입의 20%까지, 최대 10년이며, 두 달 연속 못 내면 약속 파기입니다.
[*][b]갈고리[/b]: 첩보 보고서가 소문이 아닌 확인된 비밀(우리를 칠 전쟁 명분, 공격·약화·속국화 계획)을 찾으면 그 제국의 약점을 쥡니다. 거래에서 한 번 쓰면 수락 확률 +30%p.
[*][b]참전 요청[/b]: 방위 조약·연방 동맹에게 참전을 요청하면 +25%p, 동맹이 거절하면 신문에 실립니다. 반대로 동맹이 방어 전쟁에 들어가면 우리에게 참전 요청이 오고, 거절하거나 두 달 안에 답하지 않으면 그 제국 호감 −30에 신문 보도가 나갑니다.
[*][b]약속 파기[/b]: 우리가 지급을 끊거나 못 내거나 불가침을 깨면 상대 호감 −50, 신문 보도, 그리고 상대가 이 일을 약점으로 쥡니다(그 제국의 요구를 또 거절하면 호감 −30 더). 상대가 어기면 신문에 실립니다.
[/list]

[h2]🏴‍☠️ 약탈자와 흥정[/h2]
[list]
[*]습격 예고를 받은 뒤 그 약탈자와 대화로 맞설 수 있습니다. 우리 함대가 우세 이상이면 위협해 물리칠 수 있고(대등하면 상대가 판단), 다른 제국으로 습격을 돌려 달라고 돈을 낼 수도 있습니다.
[*][b]습격 의뢰[/b]: 습격당하지 않을 때는 누구를 털면 좋을지 추천받고 습격을 맡길 수 있습니다. 대상은 일반 제국이며, 그 약탈자가 다른 습격이나 휴식 중이면 맡길 수 없습니다.
[*][b]가격[/b]: 습격 의뢰와 돌리기 모두 기본 3,000 에너지에서 ±15%로 흥정합니다. 대상이 우리 경쟁국이 아니면 1.5배입니다.
[*][b]용병 흥정[/b]: 0~15% 할인을 약속받은 뒤 1년 안에 게임 메뉴에서 그 부족의 용병을 고용하면, 할인분을 에너지로 돌려받습니다.
[/list]

[h2]👑 그레이트 칸[/h2]
[list]
[*]그레이트 칸이 나온 부족과 우주 전투를 한 적이 없고 관계가 좋았다면(그의 호감 50 이상, 또는 습격 의뢰·용병 거래 이력), 그레이트 칸의 사자가 찾아와 선물(합금 월수입 6개월치)을 줍니다.
[*]그런 제국이 태수국이 되면 [b]「칸의 총애」[/b]로 기본 자원 생산 +15%를 받습니다(태수국인 동안).
[*]그레이트 칸은 대화에서 옛정이나 옛 원한을 기억합니다. 이번 업데이트 전의 전투는 기록이 없어 싸운 적이 없는 것으로 봅니다.
[/list]

[h2]🗺️ 국경과 외교 사정을 아는 AI[/h2]
[list]
[*]게임 AI가 판단에 쓰는 정보를 대화·회의 전에 게임에서 읽어 AI에게 알려 줍니다: 맞닿은 성계와 점령지, 영유권 주장, 공동의 적, 전쟁 명분, 신뢰, 첩보 수준, 종속 관계, 사절, 은하 공동체 지위.
[*]대사관·첩보 보고, 대사와의 대화, 전쟁 선언문, 규탄 성명, 신문, 라디오에도 실제 상황이 들어갑니다. 그레이트 칸의 후계국(디아도코이·새 칸국 등)도 알아봅니다.
[*]게임 수치는 바꾸지 않고, AI 호출도 늘지 않습니다.
[/list]

[h2]🏛️ 성간 임시회의 과제[/h2]
[list]
[*]3개국 이상이 모인 성간 임시회의에서 「회의 종료」를 누르면, 주제와 가장 관련 깊은 통치자가 과제 하나를 제안합니다. 받을지는 직접 고릅니다.
[*]목표는 특정 제국과 평화 유지, 공동의 적에 선전포고, 자원 건네기, 관계 개선, 경제(수입·창고·해군 규모) 가운데 하나이고, 기한은 1~3년입니다. 진행 상황은 게임의 상황 기록(Situation Log)에 표시됩니다.
[*]보상과 불이익은 과제를 준 통치자가 정하고, 앱이 정해진 범위(퍼센트 효과는 ±15%) 안으로 맞춥니다. 성공하면 보상과 그 제국 호감 +15, 실패하면 불이익과 호감 −15입니다. 동시에 3개까지, 같은 제국에게서는 한 번에 1개입니다.
[/list]

[h2]⚙️ 그 밖[/h2]
[list]
[*]AI 연결에 [b]NanoGPT[/b]가 추가되었습니다(설정 > AI 연결).
[*][b]모드 초상화 불러오기[/b](설정 > 초상화): 켜 둔 모드의 종족 초상화를 앱이 직접 그려 초상화 목록에 넣습니다. 특수한 모양은 비슷하게만 그려질 수 있습니다.
[*]게임 언어가 한국어·영어가 아닐 때 SLLM 문구가 키 이름 대신 영어로 보입니다.
[*]한국어 표기 「대칸」을 「그레이트 칸」으로 바꿨습니다.
[/list]

앱 업데이트 안내가 나타나면 적용해 주세요. 아직 알파 버전인 만큼 예상하지 못한 문제가 있을 수 있습니다. 발견한 문제와 의견을 남겨 주세요!

===== Workshop (English, BBCode) =====
[h1]SLLM Alpha 11 — Living Empires (0.1.0-alpha.11)[/h1]

This update makes relations between empires feel more alive. You can now bundle offers in diplomatic deals, bargain with marauders, earn special treatment from the Great Khan, and take on tasks from interstellar summits, and the AI now knows the in-game diplomatic picture such as borders, fronts and claims. Most new features run on game data and the app's own calculations, so no AI is used unless you are talking to someone directly.

⚠️ [b]The mod files have changed.[/b] Please update both this mod (Workshop) and the app, and fully restart the game.

[h2]🤝 Diplomatic Deals[/h2]
[list]
[*]The Office's "Affairs of state" section has a new [b]Diplomatic Deals[/b] app. Pick a regular empire you are in contact with and bundle what to give and take into one offer. You can also open it with the "Propose a deal" button while talking in the diplomatic terminal.
[*][b]Persuasion odds[/b]: as you add items, the chance they accept and the reasons behind it (opinion, trust, power difference, deal balance and more) update instantly, and the outcome follows that chance. Pestering the same empire again in the same month lowers the chance by 10 points each time. Proposed during a conversation in the terminal, the other side answers in a line; proposed from the Office, you get the result without any AI.
[*][b]Bundled deals[/b]: combine resources (at once or monthly), open borders, ending a rivalry, a non-aggression promise, and joining our wars. Diplomatic items grow more valuable as the game years pass.
[*][b]Tribute peace[/b]: buy peace by bundling an end to the war with monthly payments. Monthly payments are capped at 20% of the payer's monthly income, for up to 10 years, and two missed months in a row break the deal.
[*][b]Hooks[/b]: when an espionage report uncovers a confirmed secret, not a rumor (a casus belli against us, or plans to attack, weaken or subjugate us), you gain leverage over that empire. Using it once in a deal adds 30 points to the chance.
[*][b]Calls to arms[/b]: asking a defensive-pact or federation ally to join your war adds 25 points, and an ally's refusal makes the papers. When an ally enters a defensive war, they call on you; refusing or not answering within two months costs −30 opinion with them and a newspaper story.
[*][b]Broken promises[/b]: if we stop or miss payments or break a non-aggression promise, their opinion drops by 50, it makes the papers, and they hold it over us (refusing their demands again costs another −30). If they break their word, the papers report it.
[/list]

[h2]🏴‍☠️ Bargaining with Marauders[/h2]
[list]
[*]After a raid warning, you can face the marauders in conversation. If your fleet is superior or better you can threaten them off (if evenly matched, they decide), or pay them to send the raid at another empire instead.
[*][b]Hiring a raid[/b]: when you are not being raided, ask whom to hit and hire a raid. Targets are regular empires, and you cannot hire them while they are on another raid or resting.
[*][b]Price[/b]: both hiring and redirecting start from 3,000 energy and can be haggled by ±15%. If the target is not our rival, the price is 1.5×.
[*][b]Mercenary haggling[/b]: get a 0–15% discount promised, and if you hire that clan's mercenaries from the game menu within a year, the discount is refunded in energy.
[/list]

[h2]👑 The Great Khan[/h2]
[list]
[*]If you never fought the Great Khan's clan in space and kept good relations (his opinion of 50 or more, or a history of hired raids or mercenary deals), the Great Khan's envoy arrives with a gift (six months of your alloy income).
[*]If such an empire becomes a satrapy, it receives [b]Khan's Favor[/b]: +15% basic resource output while it remains a satrapy.
[*]The Great Khan remembers old friendship or old grudges in conversation. Battles from before this update were not recorded, so they count as never having fought.
[/list]

[h2]🗺️ An AI That Knows the Diplomatic Picture[/h2]
[list]
[*]Before conversations and meetings, the app reads what the game AI uses to make decisions and passes it to the AI: bordering systems and occupations, claims, common enemies, casus belli, trust, intel level, subject relations, envoys, and Galactic Community standing.
[*]Embassy and espionage reports, talks with ambassadors, war declarations, condemnations, the newspaper and the radio now reflect the real situation as well. The AI also recognizes the Great Khan's successor states (Diadochi, new khanates and others).
[*]No game values change, and no extra AI calls are made.
[/list]

[h2]🏛️ Interstellar Summit Tasks[/h2]
[list]
[*]When you press "End meeting" at an interstellar summit with three or more empires, the ruler most involved in the topic proposes one task. You choose whether to accept it.
[*]Goals include keeping peace with an empire, declaring war on a common enemy, handing over resources, improving relations, or an economic target (income, stockpile or navy size), with a deadline of 1–3 years. Progress is shown in the game's Situation Log.
[*]Rewards and penalties are set by the ruler who gave the task, and the app keeps them within fixed limits (±15% for percentage effects). Success brings the reward and +15 opinion with that empire; failure brings the penalty and −15. Up to three at a time, and one per empire.
[/list]

[h2]⚙️ Other Changes[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]NanoGPT[/b] has been added as an AI provider (Settings > AI connection).
[*][b]Load mod portraits[/b] (Settings > Portraits): the app draws species portraits from your enabled mods itself and adds them to the portrait list. Some special styles may only come out approximately.
[*]When the game language is neither Korean nor English, SLLM text now appears in English instead of raw key names.
[/list]

When the app update notice appears, please apply it. This is still an alpha, so unexpected problems may occur. Please report any issues and feedback!

## 🤝 Diplomatic Deals
• The Office's "Affairs of state" section has a new **Diplomatic Deals** app. Pick a regular empire you are in contact with and bundle what to give and take into one offer. You can also open it with the "Propose a deal" button while talking in the diplomatic terminal.
• **Persuasion odds**: as you add items, the chance they accept and the reasons behind it (opinion, trust, power difference, deal balance and more) update instantly, and the outcome follows that chance. Pestering the same empire again in the same month lowers the chance by 10 points each time. Proposed during a conversation in the terminal, the other side answers in a line; proposed from the Office, you get the result without any AI.
• **Bundled deals**: combine resources (at once or monthly), open borders, ending a rivalry, a non-aggression promise, and joining our wars. Diplomatic items grow more valuable as the game years pass.
• **Tribute peace**: buy peace by bundling an end to the war with monthly payments. Monthly payments are capped at 20% of the payer's monthly income, for up to 10 years, and two missed months in a row break the deal.
• **Hooks**: when an espionage report uncovers a confirmed secret, not a rumor (a casus belli against us, or plans to attack, weaken or subjugate us), you gain leverage over that empire. Using it once in a deal adds 30 points to the chance.
• **Calls to arms**: asking a defensive-pact or federation ally to join your war adds 25 points, and an ally's refusal makes the papers. When an ally enters a defensive war, they call on you; refusing or not answering within two months costs −30 opinion with them and a newspaper story.
• **Broken promises**: if we stop or miss payments or break a non-aggression promise, their opinion drops by 50, it makes the papers, and they hold it over us (refusing their demands again costs another −30). If they break their word, the papers report it.

## 🏴‍☠️ Bargaining with Marauders
• After a raid warning, you can face the marauders in conversation. If your fleet is superior or better you can threaten them off (if evenly matched, they decide), or pay them to send the raid at another empire instead.
• **Hiring a raid**: when you are not being raided, ask whom to hit and hire a raid. Targets are regular empires, and you cannot hire them while they are on another raid or resting.
• **Price**: both hiring and redirecting start from 3,000 energy and can be haggled by ±15%. If the target is not our rival, the price is 1.5×.
• **Mercenary haggling**: get a 0–15% discount promised, and if you hire that clan's mercenaries from the game menu within a year, the discount is refunded in energy.

## 👑 The Great Khan
• If you never fought the Great Khan's clan in space and kept good relations (his opinion of 50 or more, or a history of hired raids or mercenary deals), the Great Khan's envoy arrives with a gift (six months of your alloy income).
• If such an empire becomes a satrapy, it receives **Khan's Favor**: +15% basic resource output while it remains a satrapy.
• The Great Khan remembers old friendship or old grudges in conversation. Battles from before this update were not recorded, so they count as never having fought.

## 🗺️ An AI That Knows the Diplomatic Picture
• Before conversations and meetings, the app reads what the game AI uses to make decisions and passes it to the AI: bordering systems and occupations, claims, common enemies, casus belli, trust, intel level, subject relations, envoys, and Galactic Community standing.
• Embassy and espionage reports, talks with ambassadors, war declarations, condemnations, the newspaper and the radio now reflect the real situation as well. The AI also recognizes the Great Khan's successor states (Diadochi, new khanates and others).
• No game values change, and no extra AI calls are made.

## 🏛️ Interstellar Summit Tasks
• When you press "End meeting" at an interstellar summit with three or more empires, the ruler most involved in the topic proposes one task. You choose whether to accept it.
• Goals include keeping peace with an empire, declaring war on a common enemy, handing over resources, improving relations, or an economic target (income, stockpile or navy size), with a deadline of 1–3 years. Progress is shown in the game's Situation Log.
• Rewards and penalties are set by the ruler who gave the task, and the app keeps them within fixed limits (±15% for percentage effects). Success brings the reward and +15 opinion with that empire; failure brings the penalty and −15. Up to three at a time, and one per empire.
